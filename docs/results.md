# Saved classification results

`results/classification_results.csv` is copied unchanged from the surviving thesis project. Its 36 rows were checked against **Table 14** of the submitted thesis during archive preparation. The public repository does not include the thesis PDF's personal identifiers.

The table covers three datasets, four classifier/input pipelines, and three training sources: real, DDIM, and CWGAN-GP. The four recorded metrics are accuracy, macro precision, macro recall, and macro F1. All evaluations are described in the thesis as using real held-out test data; synthetic conditions use synthetic training data rather than mixing real data into classifier training.

The recorded real-data baseline has higher macro F1 than either synthetic source for all twelve dataset/classifier pairs. This does not imply every accuracy comparison has the same ordering. For example, PathMNIST random forest accuracy is slightly higher under DDIM training (0.6435 versus 0.6347), while its macro F1 is lower (0.5377 versus 0.5452). Metric selection and class-level behavior matter.

The chart generated by `scripts/render_results.py` uses macro F1 from this saved table, not newly trained models. It intentionally shows every model/dataset group rather than selecting only the strongest synthetic outcomes. These figures should be read as recorded thesis results, not independent replication, statistical significance estimates, privacy guarantees, or evidence of clinical/operational readiness.
